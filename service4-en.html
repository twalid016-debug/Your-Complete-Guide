What Are Water Services?

Water services include digital services related to drinking water, wastewater, bills, meters and customer complaints.

Main Services

Bills

Check and pay water bills when the service is available.

Meter Readings

Submit water meter readings through available digital services.

Complaints

Submit complaints related to water or wastewater services.
