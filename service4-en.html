Water Services

Explore drinking water and wastewater services, including bills, meter readings, and complaints.

About Water Services

Water services are provided by drinking water and wastewater companies to citizens. They include connecting water to homes, issuing bills, maintenance, and receiving complaints.

Main Water Services

  • Water connection services.
  • Wastewater services.
  • Complaint services.

Holding Company for Water and Wastewater

The Holding Company for Water and Wastewater is responsible for managing its affiliated drinking water and wastewater companies in Egypt, including companies operating in the governorates.

Access Water Services

Visit the official website of the Holding Company for Water and Wastewater to explore water and wastewater services and information.

The website does not only provide billing information. It also provides information about the company, its projects, and services provided to citizens.

Main Services and Information

  1. Bill inquiry: Checking the water bill amount through the designated electronic platform.
  2. Bill payment: Paying water bills electronically.
  3. Meter reading registration: Submitting the actual water meter reading.
  4. Complaints: Reporting water outages or wastewater problems.
  5. Consumption calculation: Calculating water consumption.
  6. Customer service: Contacting the company to ask about services.
  7. Water conservation information: Learning ways to reduce water consumption and preserve water.

Benefits of Digital Services

  • Check water bills electronically.
  • Pay bills electronically.
  • Submit meter readings online.
  • Submit complaints and report problems.
  • Contact customer service.
  • Access information about water conservation.
