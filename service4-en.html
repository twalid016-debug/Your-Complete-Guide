Water Services

Learn about water services, bills, meter readings, complaints, and wastewater services.

What Are Water Services?

Water services include connecting homes to water, billing, maintenance, and complaints.

They also include wastewater-related services, customer service, and water consumption information.

Accessing Water Services

Information about drinking water and wastewater services is available through the Holding Company for Water and Wastewater.

Holding Company for Water and Wastewater
  • Bill inquiry
  • Bill payment
  • Meter reading
  • Submitting complaints
  • Consumption calculation
  • Customer service
  • Water conservation information

How to Use Water Services

  1. Open the appropriate website or service.
  2. Select the required service.
  3. Enter the required information.
  4. Follow the application steps.
  5. Track the request or complaint when available.

Benefits of Digital Water Services

  • Easy bill inquiries.
  • Online bill payment.
  • Meter reading registration.
  • Online complaint submission.
  • Access to water consumption and conservation information.
