Traffic Services

Learn about vehicle services, driving licenses and traffic violations.

What Are Traffic Services?

Traffic services include procedures related to vehicles, driving licenses, traffic violations and other available digital traffic services.

Main Services

Vehicles

Services may include vehicle license renewal, ownership procedures and vehicle data certificates.

Driving Licenses

Some services include issuing, renewing or replacing driving licenses according to the required procedures.

Violations

Citizens can access available services for checking and handling traffic violations.

How to Access

1

Open the Digital Egypt Portal.

2

Choose the appropriate traffic service.

3

Select the service and follow its official instructions.
