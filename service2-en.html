Traffic Services

Learn about vehicle services, driving licenses, traffic violations, and related digital services.

What Are Traffic Services?

Government traffic services include vehicle services, driving licenses, and traffic violation inquiries.

These services help organize traffic procedures and make vehicle and driving services easier to access.

Accessing Traffic Services

Several digital traffic services can be accessed through the Digital Egypt Portal.

  • Vehicle license renewal
  • Issuing a new vehicle license
  • Lost or damaged license replacement
  • Vehicle ownership transfer
  • Vehicle data certificate
  • Traffic violation inquiries

Driving license services also include renewal, replacement, and some inquiry services.

How to Use the Services

  1. Open the Digital Egypt Portal.
  2. Select the required service.
  3. Enter the required information.
  4. Follow the application steps.
  5. Track the service status when available.

Some first-time driving license services may require specific procedures, examinations, or personal attendance.

Benefits of Digital Traffic Services

  • Easier access to vehicle services.
  • Availability of several services online.
  • Easy traffic violation inquiries.
  • Saving time and effort for some procedures.
