Traffic Services

Explore traffic services related to vehicles, driving licenses, and traffic violations.

About Traffic Services

Traffic services are government services provided by the Ministry of Interior. They include procedures related to vehicles, driving licenses, and traffic violations.

These services aim to organize traffic, ensure vehicle validity, and make traffic procedures easier for citizens.

Vehicle License Services

  • Renewing a vehicle license.
  • Issuing a new vehicle license.
  • Issuing a replacement for a lost vehicle license.
  • Issuing a replacement for a damaged vehicle license.
  • Transferring vehicle ownership.
  • Obtaining an official vehicle data certificate.

Access Traffic Services

Digital Egypt Portal

The Digital Egypt Portal is a government platform that provides various electronic government services, including traffic services.

To access traffic services, visit Digital Egypt Portal .

You can also directly access the vehicle license violation inquiry service through Vehicle License Violation Inquiry .

Driving License Services

  • Issuing a driving license for the first time.
  • Renewing a driving license.
  • Issuing a replacement for a lost or damaged driving license.
  • Inquiring about certain driving license information.

Some services, such as obtaining a driving license for the first time, require specific procedures, examinations, and personal attendance.

Traffic Violation Services

  • Checking traffic violations registered on a vehicle.
  • Paying violations electronically using available payment methods.
  • Submitting an objection to a traffic violation.
  • Following up on objections when the electronic service is available.

Main Services on Digital Egypt

  • Renewing a vehicle license.
  • Issuing a replacement for a lost or damaged vehicle license.
  • Creating a vehicle sale power of attorney.
  • Creating a vehicle management power of attorney.
  • Checking vehicle license violations.

Benefits of Digital Services

  • Easier access to traffic services.
  • Saves time and effort.
  • Reduces the need to visit government offices for available electronic services.
  • Allows citizens to check traffic violations electronically.
  • Allows some vehicle procedures to be completed electronically.
