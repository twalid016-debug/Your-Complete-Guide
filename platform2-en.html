Japanese SPRIX Platform

About the Platform

SPRIX is a Japanese educational technology company that works in the field of education and cooperates with the Ministry of Education in Egypt in several educational programs. One of its main programs is QUREO, an interactive educational program designed to teach programming and develop students' skills in this field.

How to Use the Platform

Students can access their programming content by following these steps:

  1. Open the SPRIX platform.
  2. Enter your student code.
  3. Enter the password provided to you by the Ministry of Education.
  4. Click on Login.
  5. After logging in, you can access the programming content assigned to you.
  6. Start the required lessons and activities and follow the instructions provided on the platform.

Login

Students need their student code and the password provided by the Ministry of Education to log in to their accounts. If a student does not know their student code or password, they should contact their programming teacher at school to find out the required information or the appropriate procedure for obtaining it.

What Does the Student Learn?

  • Students learn programming in an interactive and gradual way through educational programs such as QUREO, with activities that help them understand programming fundamentals and apply them in practice.
  • Programming activities help students develop logical thinking and problem-solving skills.
  • Students can improve their skills in creating projects and applying the programming concepts they learn in practical ways.
  • SPRIX educational programs provide students with opportunities to participate in different educational activities and programs according to the available programs and their specific participation requirements.
