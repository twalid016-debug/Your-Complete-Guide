About the Platform

The E-Learning Portal is a digital educational service provided by Egypt's Ministry of Education and Technical Education.

It provides digital educational content and resources that students can use for studying and revision.

How to Access

1. Open the Ministry of Education website.

2. Go to the Educational Platforms section.

3. Choose the E-Learning Portal.

4. Select the educational stage or resources you need.

5. Start using the available educational content.
