Japanese SPRIX Platform

Learn about SPRIX and how students can use it for programming education.

About the Platform

SPRIX is an educational technology platform that supports programming education and digital learning.

It helps students develop programming skills through educational activities and learning resources.

How to Log In

  1. Open the SPRIX platform.
  2. Enter your student code.
  3. Enter your password.
  4. Continue to your learning page.

How to Use

  1. Open the available learning activity.
  2. Read the instructions carefully.
  3. Complete the required activities.
  4. Review your work.

Benefits for Students

  • Develops programming skills.
  • Supports digital learning.
  • Improves problem-solving skills.
  • Encourages independent learning.
