Digital Security

During online transactions, your data is precious. These key rules are based on NIST guidance and Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law.

01

Use a long password

Length matters more than complexity. Use at least 8 characters, ideally 12 to 16. A passphrase made of unrelated words is easy to remember, and never reuse a password across sites.

02

Turn on two-step verification

Two-step verification means a stolen password alone is not enough to enter your account. Text message codes are weaker; authenticator apps and passkeys are better.

03

Beware of phishing

A phishing site is a fake copy controlled by an attacker to steal your data. Do not click unknown links, watch for spelling mistakes in messages, type the official website address yourself, and treat any suspicious message as phishing.

04

Your rights under the data protection law

Law No. 151 of 2020 protects personal data processed electronically. It may not be collected or processed without the explicit consent of its owner, and children's data counts as sensitive. The Personal Data Protection Center oversees its enforcement.
