About the Platform

The Egyptian Knowledge Bank is a digital portal providing a wide range of educational, knowledge, and research resources.

Students can use it to access additional sources that support learning and research beyond the traditional textbook.

How to Register

1. Open the Egyptian Knowledge Bank website.

2. Choose Register or Create Account if available.

3. Choose the appropriate account type.

4. Enter the required information.

5. Complete the registration process.

6. Log in and use the resources available for your account.
