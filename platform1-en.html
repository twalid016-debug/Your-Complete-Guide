Ministry of Education Portal

About the Platform

The Ministry of Education Portal is the official website of the Ministry of Education and Technical Education. It provides students, parents, and teachers with access to educational resources, digital services, educational platforms, and other useful information.

How to Use the Platform

Follow the steps below to learn how to use the platform easily and effectively.

  1. Open the Ministry of Education Portal.
  2. Choose the educational service or platform you want to use.
  3. If the service requires a login, enter the required account information.
  4. Follow the instructions provided on the platform to access the required service.

What Can Students Benefit From?

  • Access educational resources and digital learning materials.
  • View educational content and study materials.
  • Access various educational platforms and services provided by the Ministry.
  • Stay informed about educational services and information provided by the Ministry.
