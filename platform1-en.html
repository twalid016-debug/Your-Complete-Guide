About the Platform

The Ministry of Education Portal is the official digital portal of the Ministry of Education and Technical Education.

It provides educational information, digital services, and access to different educational platforms.

How to Log In

  1. Open the Ministry of Education Portal. Ministry of Education Portal.
  2. Choose the required educational service.
  3. Enter the required information.
  4. Continue to the selected service.

How to Use

  1. Open the official portal. Ministry of Education Portal.
  2. Find the service you need.
  3. Follow the instructions.
  4. Complete the required steps.

Benefits for Students

  • Easy access to educational information.
  • Access to digital educational services.
  • Helps students find important resources.
  • Provides information in one place.
