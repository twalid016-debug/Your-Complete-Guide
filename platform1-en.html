Ministry of Education Portal

About the Platform

The Ministry of Education Portal is the official digital portal of the Ministry of Education and Technical Education.

It provides students with educational information, digital services, and access to different educational platforms.

How to Log In

Students can access the available services through the official Ministry of Education portal.

  1. Open the Ministry of Education Portal.
  2. Choose the required educational service.
  3. Enter the required information.
  4. Continue to the selected service.

How to Use the Platform

The portal can be used to access educational services and important information provided by the Ministry.

  1. Open the official portal.
  2. Find the service you need.
  3. Follow the instructions provided.
  4. Complete the required steps.

Benefits for Students

  • Easy access to educational information.
  • Access to digital educational services.
  • Helps students find important resources.
  • Provides educational information in one place.
