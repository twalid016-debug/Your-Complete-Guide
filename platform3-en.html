Madrasetna Platform

About the Platform

Madrasetna is one of the educational platforms provided by the Ministry of Education and Technical Education in Egypt. It aims to support students by providing educational content and resources that help them continue their studies and benefit from digital learning.

How to Use the Platform

Students can benefit from the educational content available on the platform by following these steps:

  1. Open the Madrasetna platform.
  2. Choose the educational content or service you want to use.
  3. Select the appropriate educational stage or grade when needed.
  4. Choose the subject or lesson you want to study.
  5. Follow the educational content and make use of the available resources.

Login

The way students access content and digital services may vary depending on the service they are using. Therefore, students should follow the login instructions provided by the specific service or platform.

What Can Students Benefit From?

  • Access digital educational content that supports their studies.
  • Benefit from additional educational resources alongside school studies.
  • Follow lessons and educational content available for students.
  • Benefit from the educational channels within the Madrasetna system, which provide educational content for different educational stages.
  • Support self-learning by providing digital educational content that students can return to whenever needed.

Tip for Students

You can use the platform as an additional source for learning and revision, and organize your time to benefit from the available content alongside your regular studies.
