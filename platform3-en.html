About the Platform

Madrasetna is an educational platform within the digital educational system of Egypt's Ministry of Education.

Students can use available digital content and resources to support lessons, revision, and learning.

What Can You Find?

Educational Lessons

Educational content that helps students follow their subjects.

Revision

Digital resources can support studying and revision.

Visual Content

Videos and visual content can support explanations of subjects.

Supporting Resources

Digital resources provide additional support for learning.

Benefits for Students

Study Outside School

Students can use digital resources to continue studying outside school.

Access to Resources

Access educational resources through internet-connected devices.

Independent Learning

Digital resources allow students to review content according to their needs.

Learning Support

Digital resources can provide additional support for studying.

How to Access

1. Open the Ministry of Education website.

2. Go to the Educational Platforms section.

3. Choose Madrasetna.

4. Select the educational stage or content you need.

5. Start using the available educational resources.
