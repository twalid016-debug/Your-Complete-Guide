Ration Services

Learn about ration cards, subsidized goods and available digital services.

What Are Ration Services?

Ration services allow citizens to benefit from the subsidized goods system using a ration card linked to family data, according to the applicable rules.

Main Services

Ration Card

Access services related to the ration card.

Adding Family Members

Some digital services may allow adding family members according to applicable rules.

Lost or Damaged Card

Digital services may provide procedures related to lost or damaged ration cards.

How to Access the Service

1

Open the Digital Egypt Portal.

2

Choose the available ration services.

3

Select the required service and follow the instructions.
