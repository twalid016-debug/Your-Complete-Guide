Ration Services

Learn about the ration system and important digital services related to ration cards and subsidized goods.

About Ration Services

Ration services are government services that allow citizens to receive subsidized goods, such as oil and sugar, using a ration card.

How the Ration System Works

  • Each family has a ration card linked to the data of its members.
  • The family receives a specific subsidy according to the applicable rules.
  • Citizens can use the card at approved ration outlets.
  • Citizens choose available goods within the value of their allocated subsidy.
  • Distribution transactions are recorded electronically for monitoring.

Access Ration Services

Digital Egypt Portal

The Digital Egypt Portal is a government platform that provides many electronic services, including ration services.

  1. Go to Digital Egypt Portal .
  2. Choose the ration services section.
  3. Select the service you need.

Ministry of Supply and Internal Trade

The website of the Ministry of Supply and Internal Trade is the official portal of the ministry responsible for the ration system in Egypt.

Visit the Ministry of Supply and Internal Trade website to find information about the ministry, its services, news, and decisions.

Main Available Services

  • Activating a ration card.
  • Issuing a replacement for a lost or damaged card.
  • Checking ration distribution.
  • Adding family members.
  • Transferring the ration card from one governorate to another.
  • Social separation.

Benefits of Digital Services

  • Access ration services electronically.
  • Easily monitor ration distribution transactions.
  • Save time and effort.
  • Access several ration card services through the Digital Egypt Portal.
  • Find official ration-related information, news, and decisions.
