Ration Services

Learn about digital ration services and important services related to ration cards.

What Are Ration Services?

Ration services allow citizens to obtain subsidized goods such as oil and sugar using a ration card.

The ration card is linked to family member data, and the family receives a subsidy value according to the applicable rules.

Accessing Ration Services

Digital ration services can be accessed through the Digital Egypt Portal.

Important services include:

  • Ration card activation
  • Replacement of lost or damaged cards
  • Checking ration distribution
  • Adding family members
  • Transferring the card between governorates
  • Social separation

How to Use Ration Services

  1. Open the Digital Egypt Portal.
  2. Choose the required service.
  3. Enter the required information.
  4. Follow the application steps.
  5. Track the service status when available.

Ration transactions are recorded electronically through approved ration outlets.

Benefits of Digital Ration Services

  • Easier access to services.
  • Less reliance on paper procedures.
  • Ability to track some services online.
  • Easy access to ration card information.
