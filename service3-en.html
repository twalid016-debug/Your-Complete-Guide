Electricity Services

Explore electricity services related to bills, meters, complaints, and digital services.

About Electricity Services

Electricity services are provided by electricity distribution companies to citizens. They include connecting electricity to homes, issuing and collecting bills, and receiving complaints.

Types of Electricity Services

  • Bills and payment services.
  • Meter services.
  • Complaints services.

Unified Electricity Services Platform

The Unified Electricity Services Platform is an electronic platform affiliated with the Egyptian Electricity Holding Company. It allows citizens to submit and follow up on electricity requests online.

Access Electricity Services

You can access the Unified Electricity Services Platform to submit and follow up on electricity requests online.

The official website of the Egyptian Electricity Holding Company also provides information about the electricity sector, affiliated companies, news, services, and electricity-related information.

Main Services Available

  1. Installing an electricity meter: Requesting a new electricity meter.
  2. Meter reading registration: Submitting the meter reading electronically.
  3. Bill payment: Paying electricity bills using available payment methods.
  4. Bill calculation: Estimating the bill according to consumption.
  5. Meter inspection: Requesting a meter inspection when there is a problem.
  6. Replacement meter: Requesting a replacement meter if the original meter is lost.
  7. Complaints and faults: Submitting complaints and reporting certain problems.
  8. Consumption data certificate: Requesting a certificate showing electricity consumption data.

Benefits of Digital Services

  • Submit electricity requests electronically.
  • Follow up on requests online.
  • Save time and effort.
  • Submit meter readings electronically.
  • Pay bills using available payment methods.
  • Submit complaints and report certain faults.
