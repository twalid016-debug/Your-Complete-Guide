Electricity Services

Learn about electricity bills, meters, complaints, and digital electricity services.

What Are Electricity Services?

Electricity services include connecting homes to electricity, issuing and collecting bills, and handling complaints.

Digital services also include several services related to meters, bills, and electricity consumption.

Accessing Electricity Services

Digital electricity services are available through the Unified Electricity Services Platform.

Unified Electricity Services Platform:

Unified Electricity Services Platform
  • Installing a new meter
  • Registering meter readings
  • Paying bills
  • Calculating bills
  • Meter inspection
  • Meter replacement in applicable cases
  • Complaints and fault reports
  • Consumption data certificates

How to Use Electricity Services

  1. Open the Electricity Services Platform.
  2. Select the required service.
  3. Enter the required information.
  4. Follow the service instructions.
  5. Track the request when available.

Benefits of Digital Electricity Services

  • Easier access to services.
  • Online meter reading registration.
  • Online bill payment.
  • Submitting complaints and fault reports.
  • Access to some consumption information.
