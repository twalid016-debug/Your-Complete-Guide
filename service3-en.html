Electricity Services

Learn about electricity bills, meters, readings and complaints.

What Are Electricity Services?

Electricity digital services provide citizens with access to services related to meters, bills, consumption readings and complaints.

Main Services

Meters

Services related to installing or replacing certain types of meters.

Bills

Check and pay electricity bills electronically when available.

Complaints

Submit and follow up on electricity service complaints.

How to Access

1

Open the Unified Electricity Services Platform.

2

Choose the service you need.

3

Enter the required information and follow the instructions.
